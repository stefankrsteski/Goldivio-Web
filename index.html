Questions

Gold and silver tracker FAQ.

What is Goldivio?

Goldivio is an offline gold and silver portfolio tracker for Android. You log your bullion bars and coins, set the spot price, and the app shows your portfolio value, invested amount and profit or loss.

Does Goldivio work without an internet connection?

Yes. Goldivio runs fully offline. There is no cloud sync, no sign-up and no server. Everything stays on your device.

Does Goldivio show live gold and silver prices?

No. Goldivio never fetches prices from the internet. You set the current gold and silver spot price per ounce yourself, and the value of your whole collection updates instantly. This is what keeps the app private and offline.

Is my gold and silver data private and secure?

Your data is encrypted and stored only on your device. It is never uploaded anywhere. You can also lock the app with fingerprint or face unlock.

What can I track with Goldivio?

Gold and silver bars and coins from any mint or refinery. For each piece you can record weight in grams or ounces, brand, series, edition, serial number, purchase price and date, and storage location, and attach receipts, certificates of authenticity and photos.

Is Goldivio free?

Goldivio is free to download on Google Play for Android. There are no accounts and no subscriptions.
