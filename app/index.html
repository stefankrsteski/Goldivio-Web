Private and local

Goldivio runs entirely in your browser. Your collection and prices are never sent to any server or third-party service, and Goldivio never looks up the current gold and silver price online.

Your collection is saved only in this browser on this device. If you clear your browser data or open Goldivio on another device, it will not be there. Press Export backup to save a copy as a file, and use Import to load it back.
